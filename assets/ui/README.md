## Prompts de création

Accueil : Create a polished wide 16:9 cartoon game welcome illustration for an original Roblox zombie survival game named Weapon Evolution. No text, no lettering, no UI, no logos. Bold navy outlines, clean rounded toy-like 3D shapes with painterly highlights, cheerful action energy. A blocky adult survivor in a teal jacket and protective gloves carrying a small stylized yellow and navy pistol pointed down runs alongside a tiny golden dog companion; three goofy green zombies behind them, ruined city and a cozy barricaded refuge in background. Coral sunset, turquoise accents, warm gold coins and tiny blue crystal fragments along path. Non-graphic, no blood, no gore. Composition designed as a horizontal header image: main characters centered with generous safe margins and wide atmospheric background, readable at small size. High quality cohesive cartoon illustration, restrained detail, appealing silhouettes.

Icônes : A production-ready transparent sprite atlas for a cartoon zombie survival game UI. Exactly eight isolated icons in a perfectly regular grid of FOUR equal columns and TWO equal rows, wide 2:1 canvas. Each icon centered in its equal grid cell, generous transparent padding; no cell borders, no text, no letters, no numbers, no background, no shadows crossing cells. Top row left to right: a shiny gold coin embossed with a simple five-point star; a glowing cyan crystal for XP; a coral-red heart with a small white medical cross; a turquoise running shoe with yellow motion lightning. Bottom row left to right: a friendly golden puppy head companion; crossed small cartoon axe and navy/yellow pistol for weapons; a gold gift box with coral ribbon; a cream checklist clipboard with three turquoise checkmarks for missions. Cohesive bold navy outlines, rounded toy-like shapes, polished 3D cartoon highlights, restrained colors warm gold turquoise coral cream, readable as small menu icons. Objects fully contained within their own cells, no overlap, consistent scale. Transparent alpha background.
